More about this chart

This is a reference chart published by the association. It is not PAPPU MAN PARLOR's own price list, and the association's address, registration details and notices are not the parlor's own business information.

The chart's own note reads नोट : राशि अदा करने वाले का ही सेवा उपलब्ध है। (As read: service is available only to the person who pays. Translation to be verified.)
